What is HabitFlow?

HabitFlow is a website that helps users build and maintain positive daily habits. Users can create personal habits, check them off each day, and monitor their consistency through streaks and completion statistics.

Who should use HabitFlow?

HabitFlow is designed primarily for students and young professionals (ages 18–35) who want to develop consistent daily routines in areas such as health, fitness, productivity, and learning. A secondary audience includes anyone pursuing personal development goals who prefers a straightforward, browser-based solution without the overhead of managing account credentials.

What information will you learn?

You will learn how to manage and track your habits, understand the importance of consistency, and discover tips for maintaining motivation. The website provides insights into habit formation and offers resources to help you stay on track with your goals.

What can users do with HabitFlow?

Users can create and customize their own habits, mark them as completed each day, and view their progress through visual representations like streaks and completion rates. The website also offers motivational tips and resources to help users stay committed to their habits.

How do the pages relate to each other?

The Home page introduces the concept and benefits of the website. The Dashboard page offers a centralized briefing of the user's habits and progress. The My Habits page allows users to create and manage their habits. The Progress page provides detailed analytics on habit completion and streaks. The Tips & Resources page offers guidance and support for maintaining consistent habits. The About page provides information about the team and the Contact page allows users to get in touch with the team.
