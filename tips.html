Tips & Resources

Why Track Habits?

Habit tracking turns "I want to get better at this" into something you can actually see and measure. When you write a habit down and check it off, you build awareness of what you do, spot patterns (good and bad), and stay accountable to your goals.

The biggest win is consistency. Small actions done regularly add up faster than big bursts of effort once in a while.

Habit Tracking Ideas

Pick habits that match your goals and make them specific:

  • Study twice per week (for 45 minutes each session)
  • Read 10 pages per day
  • Drink 6-8 glasses of water per day
  • Walk 20 minutes, 3 times per week
  • Stretch for 5 minutes after waking up
  • Practice a skill (coding, instrument, language) 3 times per week
  • Meal prep on Sundays
  • Do a 10-minute tidy-up each day
  • Track sleep: lights out by 11:30 PM
  • No-spend day once per week

How To Stay Consistent

Consistency is easier when the habit is simple, visible, and repeatable. Start small, track a realistic frequency (daily or weekly), and focus on showing up rather than being perfect.

Use HabitFlow as your "single place to check in." When you log your habits regularly, you get a clear view of your streaks and progress, which makes it easier to stay motivated.

  • Make it easy: lower the starting effort (5 minutes counts).
  • Attach it to a routine: after breakfast, before bed, after class.
  • Track the schedule you can keep (2 days/week beats 7 days/week).
  • Missed a day? Resume on the next opportunity - don't restart.

Tips to Using Habitflow

  • Choose 2-3 habits first; build momentum before adding more.
  • Write habits like actions: "Walk 20 minutes" instead of "Get fit."
  • Decide your frequency: daily, or a weekly target (like twice per week).
  • Check in often (quick daily check-ins work best).
  • Review your week in the Dashboard and Progress pages to see what's working.
  • Adjust if needed: if a habit keeps failing, make it smaller or less frequent.
